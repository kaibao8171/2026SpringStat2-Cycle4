  * 95% Confidence Interval for OR: [0.4714, 0.5549]

[BEHAVIORAL INSIGHT: SAFETY-FIRST PERSONA]
Exploratory and model analysis reveals a significant behavioral alignment:
1. Risk Reduction: Adolescents who consistently practice safety compliance 
   (wearing helmets) exhibit 0.51 times the odds of engaging in physical 
   fighting compared to non-compliant peers—a ~49% reduction in violence odds.
2. Behavioral Clustering: Compliance with safety regulations and low-violence 
   tendencies are highly co-dependent, strongly supporting the existence of a 
   systemic "Safety-First Persona" among youth.
